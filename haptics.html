Haptics test

Tap each one a few times with the phone unmuted and "System Haptics" on (Settings, Sounds & Haptics). Note which numbers give a tick. Try it in Safari, then again from the home-screen app.

1. A plain switchThe baseline. If this gives no tick, nothing will.
2. Invisible switch inside a buttonThis is the way Kiron does it. Taps counted: 0
3. Invisible switch over a plain boxTaps counted: 0
4. Hidden switch, clicked by scriptThe old trick. Apple is said to have closed it in iOS 26.5.
5. Tick a moment after the tapWould allow a tick when the streak lands. Probably silent.
6. Scroll checkPut a finger on button 2 and drag up and down. The page should scroll as normal, with no tick.
