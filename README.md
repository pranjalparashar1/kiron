# Kiron

Recall quizzes for what you read, named after Chiron, the teacher. One question per screen, short rounds of seven, and two ways to revise:

- **Early revision:** tap to answer. No typing or calculating.
- **In-depth revision:** type your answers to test yourself.

Everything runs in the browser. Answers and the activity log stay on your device.

- Make a quiz from your own PDF, slides or Word file: open the app, tap **Make a quiz**, and follow the steps (uses any AI chat).
- Add a quiz for everyone: upload a `quiz-*.json` file, add its name to `quizzes.json`, and bump `VERSION` in `sw.js`.
