# Study Buddy

Recall quizzes for what you read. Answer from memory, check yourself against the key points, and retry only what you missed. Runs entirely in the browser; answers stay on your device.

- Make a quiz from your own PDF, slides or Word file: open the app, tap **Make a quiz**, and follow the steps (uses any AI chat).
- Add a quiz for everyone: upload a `quiz-*.json` file, add its name to `quizzes.json`, and bump `VERSION` in `sw.js`.
